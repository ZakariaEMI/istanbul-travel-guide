Before you go

Your quick travel notes

Useful transit references for navigating between Istanbul's main districts.

  • T1 tram: useful for Sultanahmet, Beyazıt and Eminönü.
  • M2 metro: links Vezneciler with areas including Taksim and Şişli.
  • Ferries: explore the Bosphorus and reach the Asian side.
  • Shopping hours: check each mall's official website for holiday changes.
